# JanSeva 🇮🇳

> A citizen-first companion for navigating Indian government services.

Built for the **Build What Moves India** hackathon.

---

## 🚧 Project Status

**Currently in active development.**

JanSeva is a hackathon prototype exploring how existing Indian government
digital services can become easier for citizens to understand and navigate.

---

## 💡 The Problem

India has increasingly moved government services online.

But digitizing a government service does not necessarily make the process
easy for citizens.

A citizen may still have to:

- Figure out which government services apply to their situation
- Understand which department handles each service
- Determine whether they are eligible
- Find the required documents and information
- Understand unfamiliar government terminology
- Navigate multiple government portals
- Repeatedly enter information they already know

The problem is therefore not simply **access to digital government services**.

It is **navigating them**.

---

## 💡 Our Idea

JanSeva adds a **citizen-first layer around existing government services**.

Instead of asking:

> "Which government department do you need?"

JanSeva starts with:

> **"What changed in your life?"**

For example:

- 🏠 I moved to a new address
- 🎓 I started my first job

JanSeva then helps the citizen discover the relevant government services,
understand what they need, prepare the required information, and navigate the
official government portal.

### Government portals remain the system of record.

JanSeva does not replace them.

It acts as the **guidance and assistance layer around them**.
